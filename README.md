# AI-Powered Dynamic Mental Health Monitoring & Distress Prediction System

An AI-assisted web platform designed to support the continuous monitoring, early detection, and timely intervention of psychological distress among victims of atrocities.

## 🧠 About the Project

Victims of atrocities may experience psychological distress that develops or worsens over time. Traditional assessments can be periodic and dependent on manual follow-ups, making changes in a victim's mental well-being difficult to identify early.

Our project aims to build a secure, accessible, and human-centered system that continuously analyses multiple indicators of well-being and helps counsellors identify individuals who may require attention.

Rather than replacing mental-health professionals, the system acts as a decision-support and prioritisation tool, helping trained counsellors and authorities respond to potential distress more efficiently.

## 🎯 How It Works

The platform collects information through multiple interaction channels such as:

- 💬 Text conversations
- 🎙️ Voice interactions / voice notes
- 📋 Structured well-being check-ins
- 📊 Engagement and behavioural patterns
- 🚨 Safety or threat reports

AI/ML models analyse these signals and combine them with historical interactions to maintain a continuously updated **Dynamic Distress Score**.

The system can then classify potential risk levels, identify contributing indicators, and alert authorised professionals when intervention may be required.

## 👥 Platform Dashboards

The system is designed around three primary interfaces:

### Victim Dashboard
Provides a secure and accessible environment for victims to communicate, complete check-ins, access support resources, and interact with the system.

### Counsellor Dashboard
Helps authorised counsellors monitor assigned cases, view distress trends, understand AI-generated risk indicators, prioritise high-risk cases, and record interventions.

### Government / Authority Dashboard
Provides authorised officials with aggregated insights, regional trends, case statistics, resource requirements, and system-level monitoring while maintaining appropriate privacy controls.

## 🔐 Privacy, Ethics & Safety

Mental-health information is highly sensitive. The platform is therefore designed around privacy, security, informed consent, role-based access, and responsible AI principles.

AI-generated predictions are intended to assist trained professionals — **not diagnose individuals or independently make intervention decisions**.

High-risk cases are escalated for human review, keeping counsellors and authorised professionals at the centre of the decision-making process.

## 🌍 Expected Impact

The system aims to shift mental-health support from a primarily reactive approach toward **continuous, proactive, and human-centered support**.

Potential outcomes include:

- Earlier identification of deteriorating psychological well-being
- Faster connection between victims and counsellors
- Better prioritisation of high-risk cases
- Reduced manual screening burden
- Improved continuity of counselling and rehabilitation
- Data-supported allocation of support resources
- Better coordination between victims, counsellors, and authorities
